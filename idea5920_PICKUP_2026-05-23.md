### ThirdPartyDetector — current decision tree (per Ruben's repeated directive)

The detector now has THREE relaxed flows that DO NOT stonewall:

1. **`payment_intent_relaxed=true`** — fires when relationship_keyword ("my son/daughter/spouse/wife/husband/employee") + payment intent ("I want to pay / here's my card / cover the tuition / called in to pay"). Routes to payment-collection template: "We can take payment from a third party. Please reply with student name + email + payment method."

2. **`class_not_started_relaxed=true`** — fires when (c) vocational-name-match branch matches a student whose `Students.course_start_date > today` AND not dropped/transferred. Routes to pre-class-helper template: "The student's class has not yet started, so we are able to help you with pre-class items like registration, payment, paperwork, and scheduling questions. Once the class begins, the student must engage directly."

3. **Default not-third-party** — anyone whose email isn't in registry + body has no relationship_keyword + no name-match-to-class-started-student gets normal CS handling. This is the post-#6016 polarity flip.

The ONLY full stonewall remaining: relationship_keyword fires + NO payment intent + name match to a student whose class HAS started. That's the "parent calling about their kid's in-progress course" case which Ruben wants student-to-engage-directly.
