# 304 — TDSHS / Texas complaint response playbook (the 8/6 gold standard)

Companion to rule 302 (cross-agency doctrine). This file is the Texas-specific build sheet, derived from the 31 revision rounds Ruben walked through on the 2026-08-06 consolidated response to TDSHS Inspection 5196. Rule 302 says what never to write anywhere. This file says what to write in Texas, in what order, and in whose voice.

Read this BEFORE drafting any TDSHS response. The next set is due 2026-08-13 (two matters), 2026-08-15 (three), 2026-08-18 (one), 2026-08-19 (one).

## THE DEADLINE IS 10 CALENDAR DAYS FROM RECEIPT. NOT CLOSE OF BUSINESS.

Verified 2026-08-06 by direct extraction of the 7/27 complaint letters. The operative sentence in every TDSHS complaint letter is:

> "Within 10 days of receipt of this letter please provide this office with any and all documentation, supporting statements, paperwork and/or forms that support..."

What that language does NOT contain, and what you must never assume into it:
- No "close of business"
- No "business days" (so weekends count, in our favour on the far end)
- No time of day at all
- No "by 5:00 p.m. Central"

**Consequences for how you plan a filing:**

1. **A filing that lands at 6:30pm Central on day 10 is timely.** There is no cutoff hour in the instrument. Do not rush a response to beat a 5pm clock that does not exist.
2. **The clock runs from RECEIPT, not from the date on the letter.** Where a transmittal arrives after the close of business (two of the three 7/27 letters landed at 16:46 and 16:56 CT), receipt is arguably the next business day, which pushes the deadline out a day. The program has already put the after-hours arrival record in the filing, so that argument is preserved contemporaneously.
3. **Thoroughness beats speed, every time.** Ruben, 2026-08-06: "rather than a rushed specific response for today, a thorough response would be better." A rushed filing creates admissions that live forever. A filing that lands late in the day on the due date creates nothing.
4. **Never volunteer the send time.** If timeliness is ever raised, the answer is their own 10-day-from-receipt language plus the after-hours arrival of the transmittals. Do not pre-emptively explain or apologise for the hour (rule 02).

## LENGTH: they did not expect it, and that is the point

The Department's letter asks for "a written statement regarding the alleged complaint." Their process is built for a two-page reply. A 13-page consolidated Response answering three control numbers is not what the intake queue is designed to absorb.

That asymmetry is a feature, not an overreach, for three reasons:

1. **Length forces escalation.** A short letter gets closed by the coordinator. A document that raises a threshold objection, reserves under Government Code Chapter 2001, and records a procedural ledger has to be read by someone with authority to evaluate whether the file survives a contested case.
2. **Every paragraph is doing defensive work.** Nothing in the filing is padding. If a section cannot be traced to a specific defense, an objection, a reservation, or a fact the record needs contemporaneously, cut it.
3. **The volume itself answers the volume.** Eleven control numbers in twenty days, each demanding a separate written statement. A licensee who answers each in two pages is a licensee who has conceded that the aggregate is normal.

**The one caution:** length is only an asset when the document is internally consistent. A long filing that contradicts itself, or that contradicts an accompanying letter, hands them more surface area, not less. That is why the accompanying-document consistency gate in rule 302 exists.

## THE STRUCTURAL DECISION: consolidate, always


One consolidated Response answering every matter transmitted on the same date, plus a short per-matter letter that incorporates it by reference. Never a standalone letter per control number that repeats the facts.

Reason, in Ruben's framing: several separately-worded accounts of one set of events, filed on one date, is the licensee handing the agency inconsistencies to find. A single reply avoids that. Where matters were transmitted on the same date and concern the same program and period, say so and answer them together.

The per-matter letter is thin by design. It carries: the control number, the transmittal receipt time, whatever is genuinely particular to that matter, and one paragraph incorporating the consolidated Response in full. It must not restate the threshold objection, the reservation, or the procedural note.

**If the agency declines consolidation** (TDSHS did, on 8/4-8/5, offering extensions one control number at a time instead), record the decision and its practical consequence, then keep consolidating anyway. See the CONSOLIDATION DECLINED paragraph below.

## SECTION ORDER (use this skeleton)

## THE PUBLIC-INTEREST OPENING (Texas-specific, use it)

Texas is short of certified EMS personnel. Rural counties staff ambulances short, urban systems compete for the same people, and every candidate who completes and passes the National Registry is one more person available to answer a call.

The move: the outcome the transmittals anticipate is one the program has every institutional reason to prevent and no reason whatever to cause. The program is in the business of certifying EMTs. A student who does not test is a student the program failed to deliver on, and the cost falls on the program, the student, and the Texas EMS agencies that would have hired them.

Why this works: it reframes the alignment of interest before the agency reads a single allegation, and it is unanswerable, because it is true and structural rather than defensive.

**Watch the rule-302 trap here.** This paragraph is where the banned recital sneaks back in. Write "the outcome these transmittals anticipate" and STOP. Never write "the outcome these transmittals anticipate, that a student will be unable to complete the externship and test, is..." The subordinate clause restates the charge in our voice.

## THE OUTSET LEDGER (the highest-value structure in the whole filing)

A bulleted list at the FRONT, not the close, of every procedural fact drawn from the correspondence between the parties, each with its date. Each bullet is a fact. The program "makes no allegation as to purpose and draws no conclusion from any of them." Each is set out in full in the Procedural Note at the close.

The bullets from 8/6, reusable as a checklist for the next set:

- Correspondence sent to an address that does not exist
- Correspondence sent outside the agreed point of contact
- Response periods measured from transmittal rather than from receipt
- A response clock ran on a transmittal the agency itself stated was incomplete
- Extension requested once, in writing, denied in approximately twelve hours
- Extensions later offered, one control number at a time
- Transmittals delivered outside business hours
- A written statement demanded about a private accreditor, on a same-day timeline
- Course applications returned while these matters were pending
- Consolidation declined
- Intake information requested and not provided
- Scope of the site entry not confirmed
- Requirements asserted without identifying any statute, rule, or provision of the administrative code, as a continuing pattern
- Control number discrepancy not resolved
- Every communication that actually reached the program was answered on or ahead of its deadline

**Front-loading is the point.** Ruben moved these from the close to the front deliberately. A reader who starts here reads every subsequent allegation through the procedural record. A reader who finds them at the end has already formed a view.

## THE TONE RULES (Ruben's voice in a regulator filing)

This is the part that is hard to reconstruct from the doctrine alone. It is not the casual iMessage voice of rule 01. It is a distinct register.

**State, do not characterise.** "The program states these times without characterising them and computes nothing from them." Give the agency the fact and let it draw the inference. Never draw it for them.

**Explicitly disclaim the inference you are obviously inviting.** "The program notes, without asserting purpose, that the Department is in contact with EMS providers across the state and was in a position to assist with placement capacity in this market had it elected to, and that what the Department directed to the program in these weeks were requests for records." The disclaimer is what makes the observation sayable. Without it the sentence is an accusation; with it, it is a record.

**Use their own words as the load-bearing element.** The CAPCE same-day-demand paragraph works because the agency "stated that it had not put a timeline on the original request, and asked for the statement by the end of that day or the following day." No adjective needed. Quote the admission and stop.

**Record verbatim, take no position.** "The Department response stated that its request for information is not dependent upon the respondent ability to independently verify the Department complaint-intake process. The program records that response verbatim." That sentence does more damage quoted flat than any rebuttal would.

**Say why you are saying it.** Nearly every structural paragraph names its own purpose: "The program states this not in mitigation of any allegation but because a program indifferent to whether its graduates were competent would have chosen the opposite in each instance." "The program raises this as a factual observation about capacity, not as an objection to any individual request."

**Contemporaneous-record framing.** Close reservations with: "The program states these reservations now, in this Response, rather than raising them later. An objection recorded when the events occur is part of the contemporaneous record. The same objection raised for the first time at a later stage is characterised as an afterthought. The program prefers the record."

**Concede the reasonable point immediately, then narrow it.** "Each individual request the Department has made is, taken alone, a request a licensee can answer. The program says that plainly and does not suggest otherwise. What the program records here is the aggregate." This is the single most disarming move in the document.

**Understate, never overstate.** "That work is ongoing rather than complete, and the program states it in that form rather than overstating it."

**No adjectives of grievance.** The words burdensome, unreasonable, excessive, retaliatory, harassment, and pattern-of-conduct do not appear. "The program does not characterise the volume, does not describe it as burdensome or unreasonable, and does not suggest any purpose behind it. It states the number."

## THE DESIGN DEFENSE (the Texas externship argument, reusable verbatim)

This is the substantive core and it applies to every placement-delay complaint in Texas.

1. **The live-site standard is structural, not chosen.** The supervised clinical and field component is required of every approved EMS education program in Texas. The dependence on live-site capacity is a feature of EMS education in this state, not a condition this program created or can remove.

2. **We depart from the state minimum upward.** Passing standard above the minimum, in-person psychomotor testing with examiner sign-off rather than a director attestation, no credentialing on simulated patient contacts. Each is voluntary, each makes completion harder. "A program indifferent to whether its graduates were competent would have chosen the opposite in each instance."

3. **Externship after didactic is a design feature, not a delay.** A program requiring hours during instruction makes every student dependent on site capacity arriving in a particular week, the one variable the program cannot control. Separating what the student controls (coursework) from what only a site can supply (hours) is deliberate. The completion window extends past didactic completion for exactly that purpose.

4. **Director discretion to extend absorbs the circumstance.** Built in because site availability is external. A delay in securing a placement does not become a loss to the student while that authority remains available and is used. Exercised on academic judgment, "not because any provision requires it."

5. **A request is not a schedule.** Preferred dates are a request, not an agreement. The obligation is to schedule an externship and the program discharges it by working the placement, not by satisfying any particular date.

6. **Summer peak is seasonal and structural.** EMS initial education in Texas concentrates enrollment in late spring and early summer; cohorts reach the externship stage together; affiliated sites do not expand to match. "The program states this as context, not as an excuse, and it does not offer it in place of the work."

7. **The priority-order rule.** Confirmed capacity is finite. A confirmed slot goes to a student who has completed every other requirement and is ready to begin. A student with didactic work outstanding "is not passed over as a matter of preference"; the program will not hold a confirmed slot against unfinished coursework, because that denies the slot to a student who has met every requirement. "No provision identified in these transmittals prohibits the program from prioritising students who have completed all other requirements, and the program does not concede that any provision requires it to do otherwise."

Point 7 is the strongest single paragraph in the filing. It converts what looks like neglect into a defensible allocation rule, and it does so without blaming the student.

## THE TEXAS CITATION SET

- **Health and Safety Code Chapter 773** and **25 TAC Chapter 157** are the governing authorities. Demand identification of the provision within them, every time.
- **25 TAC 157.32(p)(21)(F)** — program's duty to inform the Department of physical and mailing address. Cite when asking for mail service.
- **25 TAC 157.32(u)(1)(A) and (u)(3)** — Department directs notice to the address in its current records. Cite alongside the above: "The program is asking that the record the rule refers to be the record used."
- **Government Code Chapter 2001** (APA) — reservation of rights.
- Never explain a provision back to the Department. Rounds 22-25 removed a paragraph doing exactly that. Cite it, state your position, stop.

## THE THRESHOLD OBJECTION (Texas form)

"Timely externship scheduling" and "adequate student support" are not standards established by Chapter 773 or Chapter 157, and no transmittal identifies a provision that defines one.

Then, in order:
- Request the specific provision and the measure of compliance, per matter
- State you cannot respond on the merits absent a stated standard
- Do not concede any such standard exists
- Note Chapter 157 prescribes no interval for securing a placement, no schedule for didactic relative to placement, and no defined level of support
- Invite them to identify any provision they believe does
- Answer the facts notwithstanding, and reserve the objection in full

**Use "statute, rule, or provision of the administrative code," never the bare word "citation."** Round 21. A "citation" is vague and reads as a debating point. Naming the three instruments makes it a legal demand.

**The pattern extension.** Do not confine the objection to the present matters. Record that a requirement was previously asserted against the program by reference to a TAC provision whose text did not state the requirement, and that consequences were attached anyway. Offer the specifics on request. Then draw the distinction explicitly: an agency reading an ambiguous provision is ordinary administration and unobjectionable; a requirement appearing in correspondence but not in any cited text is a different thing, because the licensee has no notice, no opportunity to conform, and no way to test it short of a contested case over a rule never adopted.

## THE ARRIVAL RECORD (Section D)

List every Departmental communication with date, count, and Central time of receipt. Then: "The program states these times without characterising them and computes nothing from them. It records them because each arrival required the same staff who work student placements to stop that work and assemble a response, and because the weeks in which they arrived are the weeks in which the placements at issue were being secured."

The capacity point: "The staff who prepare these responses are the same staff who process student placements, grievances, and completion documentation. Time spent answering concurrent requests is time not spent on the student-facing work that several of these matters concern."

**Rule 302's no-day-counts rule still binds.** Record arrival times. Never compute an interval from them. The one exception the 8/6 filing makes is the extension denial ("approximately twelve hours"), which is safe because the number damages the agency, not us.

## PER-MATTER LETTERS: incorporate, never duplicate

The 8/6 set exposed the failure mode. The three per-matter letters had been written on 8/1 under the pre-302 confessional doctrine and were never brought forward. They contradicted the consolidated Response on its load-bearing point: the consolidated filing says no provision "conditions such an adjustment on prior consultation" and the program "does not concede that any of those obligations exists," while the Smith letter listed as a corrective measure "Date changes on a student placement request require the student agreement." That is a self-inflicted admission sitting in the same envelope as the denial.

Rewritten 2026-08-06. Everything below was stripped and must never reappear in a Texas filing:

| Stripped | Why |
|---|---|
| "Transmission Defect Identified and Corrected" heading | Names a program-side failure in our own voice |
| "Fleet wide the defect covered 749 threads across 483 students" | Internal diagnostic quantified and handed over; also a day-count-equivalent aggregate |
| "The requests were recorded, and the recording was treated as equivalent to delivery. It was not." | Confession |
| "course total 96.4 against a passing threshold of 80" | Numeric grades, and "course total" is a gradebook rollup, banned by 302 |
| Option A / Option B full-refund remedy | Volunteers a refund the agency never demanded, and implies liability |
| "Date changes require the student's agreement" | Concedes the exact obligation the consolidated Response denies |
| "designated coordinator as a single point of contact" | Creates a commitment; also contradicts the "assigned to a named coordinator on the date of submission" record framing |
| "a placement request unconfirmed at fourteen days is escalated" | Volunteers an interval where the whole defense is that no interval exists |
| Named site "Mineral Wells Fire Department" | Third-party site named in a regulator filing for no benefit; use "a fire-department site operating under an executed affiliation agreement on file" |
| "Thank you for providing the complainant identity" | Thanks them for something the program has separately objected to not receiving in full |

**The rule:** never let a per-matter letter volunteer an interval, a commitment, a numeric grade, a named third party, or a remedy the agency did not demand. If the consolidated Response denies an obligation, no accompanying letter may adopt it.

## EVIDENCE THAT WINS: third-party attestation

The single strongest factual move in the 8/6 filing is preceptor-signed documentation on four separate dates. A preceptor signature is a third party attesting the student was present and supervised on the date recorded. That is not the program's word.

**Verify signature images before relying on them.** Signature payloads are base64 PNGs in `ExternshipFormSubmission.preceptor_signature`. A naive dark-pixel sample undercounts because PNG rows are filtered; decode and unfilter properly, then measure ink pixels and the stroke bounding box. On 2026-08-06 all eight payloads across the four dates showed 1,000 to 5,300 ink pixels with distinct bounding boxes, confirming handwritten strokes rather than blank or auto-populated canvases.

**Count the dates, not the documents.** "Preceptor-signed documentation on four separate dates" is stronger and safer than "seventeen submissions," because seventeen invites a count-based reply and four dates is the fact that matters.

## LINKS AND ENCLOSURES

**Zero URLs in the filing.** No drive.google, no portal links, nothing auth-gated. Verify with a newline-flattened extraction, not a raw grep: `pdftotext file.pdf - | tr '\n' ' ' | grep -c 'drive.google'`. Line wrapping in pdftotext output produces false zeros on a naive grep.

Supporting documents are registered to `compliance_source_documents` against every applicable control number and served through the authenticated resolver at `personnel/compliance_ref.php`. They are not transmitted unless the Department requests them.

## PRE-TRANSMISSION SWEEP (Texas additions to the rule-302 list)

Run against a flattened extraction:

```
pdftotext F.pdf - | tr '\n' ' ' > /tmp/v.txt
grep -ciE '<every student surname>' /tmp/v.txt          # 0
grep -c 'drive.google\|http' /tmp/v.txt                 # 0
grep -c $'\u2014' /tmp/v.txt                            # 0 em dash
grep -ci 'course total\|no longer accredited' /tmp/v.txt # 0
grep -ciE 'alleges|states that|asserts that' /tmp/v.txt  # inspect every hit
grep -ci 'transmission defect\|749' /tmp/v.txt           # 0
grep -ciE 'Attorney General|TPIA|retaliat' /tmp/v.txt    # 0
grep -ci 'single point of contact' /tmp/v.txt            # 0
pdfimages -list F.pdf | grep -c '256    76'              # >= 1 signature
```

Plus: no third-party site named, no numeric grade, no placement-rate figure, no interval volunteered.

## WHAT NOT TO ADOPT FROM THEIR FRAMING

Round 30 caught the program describing the CAPCE request as one for a statement "detailing why it is no longer accredited." That adopts the agency's premise in our voice under Ruben's signature. Neutral form: "a written statement concerning its standing with" the accreditor.

Generalise: never restate a request in terms that presuppose the fact in dispute. Describe the request, not its premise.

**Two consequences for the next set:**

1. **The old per-matter letters are in TDSHS's hands.** The confessional content listed in the strip-table above (the transmission-defect heading, the 749/483 count, numeric course totals, the Option A/B refund, the date-changes concession, the single-point-of-contact commitment, the fourteen-day interval, the named site) is on their file. Do not repeat it, but also do not pretend it is not there. Never retract in writing; that draws attention to it.
2. **The rewritten letters are the template for 8/13 onward,** not a correction to be re-sent for 8/6. Do not send a superseding version of a filing that has already landed. That creates a second document on one matter, which is exactly what consolidation exists to prevent.

**Standing rule from this:** the accompanying-document consistency gate (rule 302) must run BEFORE transmission, not after. On 8/6 it ran after. That is the whole lesson.
